Return between ZERO and FIVE findings for the entire lesson. Never try to fill the quota. A fully correct lesson must return zero findings.

- minor alignment or spacing preferences;
- subjective style improvements;
- a concern that cannot be seen in the supplied frames;
- more than one finding for the same underlying defect;
